FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt?

We apply a topical numbing cream thoroughly before the treatment and add more during the procedure to minimize pain. Most clients describe it as a slight stinging sensation.

Can I go back to my daily routine right after?

Yes, right away. For 3–7 days there may be light flaking and the color may look darker, after which it settles into a natural shade.

Do I need a touch-up?

Some pigment may fade as the skin heals, so a touch-up within 4–8 weeks is required. One touch-up is included in the price; please book it at least 2 weeks before your visit.

What should I avoid after the treatment?

For 7 days, avoid saunas, swimming and heavy exercise, and do not rub the area or pick at any flakes. Apply the provided healing ointment 2–3 times a day.

How long does a lash lift last?

It depends on your lash growth cycle, but it usually lasts about 4–6 weeks. Please avoid washing with water and wearing mascara on the day of the treatment.
